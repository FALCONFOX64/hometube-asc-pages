HomeTube

Private family videos on this iPad — Support

HomeTube is a calm, private place for your family’s videos. Large tiles and oversized Play, Pause, Replay, Favorite, More, and All Done controls. No feeds, no accounts, and no ads added by HomeTube. A caregiver can optionally link YouTube videos, channels, or playlists; those play in an embedded YouTube player and may include ads shown by YouTube.

How it works

  • A caregiver unlocks settings with a PIN and imports clips from Photos or Files.
  • Imported videos are copied into HomeTube’s Documents folder on this device for offline playback.
  • Optionally, a caregiver can link YouTube videos, channels, or playlists. These stream from YouTube and need an internet connection.
  • Optional spoken button labels and session auto-stop help keep playtime bounded.
  • Guided Access tips help keep the iPad in child mode.

Privacy in one line

Home videos, settings, the PIN, and watch-time reports stay on the device; linked YouTube content involves YouTube and Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. See the HomeTube Privacy Policy.
