HomeTube

Private family videos on this iPad — Support

HomeTube is a calm, private place for your family’s videos. Large tiles and oversized Play, Pause, Replay, Favorite, More, and All Done controls. No feeds, no ads, no accounts, and no streaming from the internet.

How it works

  • A caregiver unlocks settings with a PIN and imports clips from Photos or Files.
  • Videos are copied into HomeTube’s Documents folder on this device for offline playback.
  • Optional spoken button labels and session auto-stop help keep playtime bounded.
  • Guided Access tips help keep the iPad in child mode.

Privacy in one line

Everything stays on the device. See the HomeTube Privacy Policy.
