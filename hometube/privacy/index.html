HomeTube Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026 · Matches the App Privacy Nutrition Labels and PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy shipped with HomeTube 1.0

This policy describes the HomeTube iOS / iPadOS app published under Apple Developer account caustin@me.com (bundle id com.family.hometube). It is only about the app on your device.

On-device only

HomeTube keeps your library on this iPad (or iPhone). Imported videos are copied into the app’s Documents folder for offline playback. Nothing leaves the device for HomeTube features — there is no HomeTube cloud sync, no HomeTube account, and no HomeTube server that receives your videos or settings.

No accounts, analytics, or ads

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics SDKs, advertising pixels, or tracking domains.
  • NSPrivacyTracking is false. HomeTube does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes is empty — HomeTube does not collect personal data off the device for analysis, advertising, or sale.

Photos and Files import

When a caregiver chooses Import, HomeTube reads a video you select from Apple Photos or the Files picker and copies that file into HomeTube’s local Documents storage. The original stays where it was. HomeTube does not upload those files.

PIN and caregiver settings (local)

Caregiver PIN, spoken-label preference, control layout bias, auto-stop minutes, and related flags are stored locally on the device (including Apple’s UserDefaults API). In Apple’s privacy manifest this is declared as accessed API category UserDefaults with reason CA92.1 (app functionality). These settings are not sent to a HomeTube server.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We do not use your videos for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not require an internet connection for core playback after videos are imported.
